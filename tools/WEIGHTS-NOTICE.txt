MIDI-DDSP pretrained weights
============================

The files in weights/midi_ddsp_model_weights_urmp_9_10 are the unmodified
pretrained MIDI-DDSP checkpoints (Expression Generator step 5000, Synthesis
Generator step 50000) published by the MIDI-DDSP authors on the `models`
branch of https://github.com/magenta/midi-ddsp, a repository licensed under
the Apache License, Version 2.0 (http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).

Copyright 2022 The MIDI-DDSP Authors.

Paper: Yusong Wu et al., "MIDI-DDSP: Detailed Control of Musical Performance
via Hierarchical Modeling", ICLR 2022.

The models were trained on the URMP dataset
(https://labsites.rochester.edu/air/projects/URMP.html); check its terms before
redistributing the weights or audio made with them.
